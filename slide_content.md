# Innovation Navigator: Accelerating Client Transformation

### Your AI-powered copilot for navigating the 8-stage innovation journey

---

**Description:**
A centralized platform designed for EY consultants to manage client innovation missions from kick-off to scale. It combines a structured methodology with an intelligent recommendation engine to surface the right services, frameworks, and experts at every stage of the project lifecycle.

**Key Features:**
*   **Structured 8-Stage Journey:** Guided workflow from *Kick-off & Scoping* to *Closure & Continuity*, ensuring methodological consistency.
*   **AI-Powered Recommendations:** Smart engine analyzes project context (industry, pain points, goals) to suggest high-impact services with confidence scoring.
*   **Holistic Client View:** Unified profile tracking digital maturity, strategic objectives, and portfolio progress across multiple projects.
*   **Expert Connection:** Automatically maps recommended services to internal experts (e.g., *Product Strategy, UX Design, Immersive Tech*).
*   **Interactive Innovation Catalog:** Browse and deploy 40+ curated assets, frameworks, and delivery tools tailored to client needs.
